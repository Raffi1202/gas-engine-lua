# Gas Engine ECU

> **Pre-release:** Gas Engine ECU 1.11.0 / Carb 0.11.0 are tested in the Ethos simulator; the transmitter test on the X18RS follows. Use at your own risk.

**Gas Engine ECU** is the widget for **fuel-injected engines with an ECU**. It shows cylinder temperatures (1–4 cylinders), RPM, fuel pressure, ECU voltage, tank, up to three batteries, power switch and reception, warns with colour, voice and vibration and keeps a list of your flights.

**Only in the ECU widget:** fuel pressure (with a green band on the gauge, warning and alarm while the engine runs) and ECU voltage (status bar, warning and alarm).

**You need:** a FrSky radio with Ethos 26.1 or newer and a colour display, a model with telemetry (at least RPM or cylinder temperature) and a three-position switch for off / ignition / choke.

Upgrading from Motoren ECU (up to 1.9.0): see [Installation › Upgrading](Installation.md#upgrading-from-motoren-ecu--vergaser).
